About Me

The writer you're about to hire!

I'm a technical writer who specializes in making complex technology understandable for everyone. With a background in IT support, publishing, and journalism, I excel at creating clear, user-focused documentation. I reduce customer frustration and grant users greater control over the tools they use. Whether it's API guides, tutorials, or prompt engineering, I translate "technobabble" into plain English.

My first job out of high school was as a media services technician. This was at the New York City College of Technology while I was a student there. My experience in IT support involved successfully guiding non-technical users through troubleshooting, which is technically when my technical writing career started.

I view my role as that of a translator, a skill I've honed since childhood. As the child of an immigrant family, I was often the designated translator for English and legalese. It's a skill I've carried with me and now apply professionally to bridge the gap between complex technology and the people who use it.
